About

Olayinka Ajibola Oyeniyi began his career in 2002 as a mechanical engineer. Over six years he worked as a project, piping and pipeline engineer across Nigerian oil, gas and energy services companies, then took the lead at Prec Pearl Limited in November 2008.

Prec Pearl is an ISO-certified indigenous engineering firm founded in 2003, headquartered in Yaba, Lagos, with an office in Accra, Ghana. It designs, installs, develops and maintains infrastructure, mainly for Nigeria's telecommunications sector.
